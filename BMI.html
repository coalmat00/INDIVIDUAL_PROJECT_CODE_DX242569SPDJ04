What does BMI mean?

BMI compares weight with height. It is a simple screening measurement, not a diagnosis of health or body composition.

Underweight < 18.5
Normal range 18.5-24.9
Overweight 25.0-29.9
Obesity ≥ 30.0
Calculation

BMI = weight (kg) / [height (metres) x height (metres)]. Adult BMI categories are used.
